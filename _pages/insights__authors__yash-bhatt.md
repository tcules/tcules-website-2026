# Yash Bhatt, Product Designer at Tcules

Product Designer, Tcules

Yash Bhatt is a Product Designer at Tcules. Yash writes guides on SaaS onboarding, landing pages, ecommerce, UX audits, marketing automation software and inclusive design.

The guides follow how a product earns confidence at each step. They run from the ad that brings a visitor to a landing page, through search and checkout, to onboarding and repeat use.

Other pieces cover commissioning a UX audit a team can act on, and telling UX, CX and design thinking apart. One treats cognitive bias as a risk in the team's own judgement.
