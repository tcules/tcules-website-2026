## Featured SaaS UX case studies: modernisation, AI and an MVP {#featured}

### Benchmark Gensuite: modernising an enterprise suite

A long-running suite of environment, health, safety and sustainability software had to look and work like modern software, while customers kept the habits they rely on. We tried new interface patterns in one busy feature, as working prototypes in code, so Benchmark's engineers could check them before wider rollout.

**Our role:** modernisation, design systems and coded prototypes. [Read the Benchmark Gensuite case](/case-studies/benchmark-gensuite-enterprise-suite-modernisation)

### ConnectX: an AI building-energy workspace

A building-energy product where asking the AI a question opens a workspace with the right data and tools. Its production-ready module has been live since late 2025.

**Our role:** AI Product UX, the coded demo and the production-ready module. [Read the ConnectX case](/case-studies/ai-native-building-energy-product-connectx)

### Doodle: finding meeting times that will hold

An empty slot in a calendar is no promise someone will turn up. We studied which meetings people really keep, and designed the MVP to show how likely each time is to hold.

**Our role:** research and product design. [Read the Doodle case](/case-studies/doodle-case-study-automating-enterprise-meeting-scheduling)

## Questions about our product design case studies {#faq}

### Which case study is closest to our product?

Start with the state your product is in: new, established, adding AI, or needing to be built. Each group above has a "Best for" column, so pick the row that sounds like your situation. If nothing fits closely, tell us about your product and we will point you to the nearest work.

### Has Tcules designed new SaaS products?

Yes. Doodle's enterprise scheduling MVP, Auxentios' industrial quoting product and Novus Insights' survey tool all started as new products. Each case shows the research and the choices that shaped the first release. [New product case studies](#new-products)

### Has Tcules modernised established enterprise software?

Yes. For Benchmark Gensuite, we tried new interface patterns in one busy feature as working prototypes in code, so its engineers could check them before wider rollout. Gold Image Printing's ordering site and production system were redesigned together. [Established product case studies](#established-products)

### What AI product UX work has Tcules done?

ConnectX's building-energy workspace, where a question opens the right data and tools, has a production-ready module live since late 2025. Eden AI, BuildTwin and Omny AI show AI features people can steer, challenge and approve. [AI product case studies](#ai-products)

### Did Tcules build the software in these cases?

Yes, in several. Our developers built CuramCare's frontend, the GT Tools store on Shopify and The Chain Hut's Magento store. In others, such as Dealpath, Tcules designed the work and the client's own team owned the build. Each case states who did what.
