# Product design guides for the job your team is working on

Ten practical product design guides for teams building and improving software. Each takes one job, from choosing a design studio to onboarding, a UX audit or a landing page, and shows how to do it well. Most guides include an example from our own client work or our own site.

## Guides to choosing a design partner {#partner}

**[How to choose a product design agency and test the fit](/insights/guides/how-to-choose-a-product-design-agency).** Six things to check before you hire one, and how a studio compares with freelancers, in-house teams and development companies.

**[Best SaaS design agencies in 2026: eight studios compared by fit](/insights/guides/best-saas-design-agencies).** Eight studios side by side, from their own websites: where each is based, what it covers beyond design, how work starts and which buyer each suits best.

## SaaS product design guides: onboarding, engagement and mobile {#saas-value}

**[SaaS onboarding best practices: take each role to first value](/insights/guides/saas-onboarding-strategies-to-increase-customer-retention).** Define first value separately for the buyer, the administrator and the daily user, then map each one's route from sign-up to real work.

**[SaaS user engagement: measure the behaviour that shows customers getting value](/insights/guides/saas-engagement-and-subscription-decisions).** Decide which repeated behaviour shows a customer getting value before you reach for trials, gamification or personalisation. Then diagnose why usage fell.

**[Does my SaaS need a mobile app? A moment-by-moment test](/insights/guides/saas-does-your-app-need-a-mobile-version).** Find the moments of work that happen away from a desk and cannot wait, then choose the kind of mobile product that suits them.

## Marketing automation and operations software {#operations}

**[Marketing automation UX that shows operators what each campaign will do](/insights/guides/marketing-automation-software-design-best-practices).** Show operators who receives what, why, and who approved it, before any message goes out.

## Practical UX guides for reviewing and improving a product {#improve}

**[How to use a UX audit: from first question to ranked plan](/insights/guides/how-to-use-a-ux-product-audit).** Choose the question before the method, look beyond the screens, and write each finding so a team can own it, put it in order and test it.

**[Inclusive product design for older and disabled people, from standard to release](/insights/guides/inclusive-product-design-for-older-and-disabled-people).** Set a testable accessibility baseline, work with the people who meet the barriers, and check that removing one barrier does not create another.

## Ecommerce UX guides: stores and landing pages {#commerce}

**[Ecommerce UX best practices: find where shoppers lose confidence](/insights/guides/ecommerce-product-experience-decision-guide).** Follow a shopper from discovery to delivery, see where confidence drops, and measure the whole path, returns and support included.

**[Landing page design best practices, from the ad's promise to confirmation](/insights/guides/landing-page-design-guide-to-boost-conversions).** Design from the ad, email or search result that brought the visitor, through the page, to the confirmation after they act.

## Product design guides FAQs {#faq}

### Where can I find practical guides on SaaS UX?

Start with the three SaaS guides on this page: onboarding, user engagement and whether your product needs a mobile app. Each takes one job and gives a method, a worked example and a short FAQ. The UX audit guide suits teams that first need to find where the product breaks.

### Is there a guide to choosing a product design agency?

There are two, and they do different jobs. How to choose a product design agency gives you the checks, the first-call questions and a small test project. The comparison of SaaS design agencies applies that thinking to eight named studios, read from their own websites.

### Which guides cover SaaS onboarding and engagement?

The onboarding guide covers the route from sign-up to first value for each role. The engagement guide picks up after that, with metrics tied to value and a way to diagnose why customers drift. Read them in that order if accounts stall early.

### Are there guides on inclusive product design?

Yes. The inclusive product design guide covers the accessibility standard to meet, planning from the barrier a person meets, assisted use and a release checklist. It is written for products used by older and disabled people, and it helps everyone else who uses them.

### How do these guides differ from the articles?

A guide walks through one job step by step and ends in something you can use, such as a checklist or a template. An article takes a position on a question and argues it. Browse the articles when you want the argument behind a method.
