## Every project starts from your product and your users {#product-knowledge}

Bring an idea, a prototype or a product with years behind it. We learn it first, with the people who know it best.

**A new product.** We find who it's for and what to build first, before anyone draws a screen.

**A prototype.** We work out what it proves and what it needs before customers rely on it.

**An established product.** We learn the reasons behind its quirks: user habits, earlier choices and technical trade-offs.

Your product managers, engineers and domain specialists bring the context, and customers join when the question calls for it.

### A pilot project, at a fixed price

Optional: one flow, one hard question or an audit, with the price agreed up front.

OctiFi's first design pilot grew into continuing work on onboarding, loan issuance, new features and a second market.

### A defined project, scoped and priced first

A first release, a redesign or a design system, with roles and milestones set before we start.

### An ongoing partnership, at a fixed monthly fee {#ongoing}

A senior designer leads, specialists join as the work needs, and you can change the team with two weeks' notice.

## Questions about outsourcing UX design {#faq}

### How do I outsource UX design for a SaaS product?

Send a few lines about the product and what needs to change, and we'll reply to arrange a call. After the call, you get a written proposal naming the work, the people and the price. A pilot project is a sensible first step, and it is optional.

### Is a UX design retainer or a fixed project better?

A defined project suits clear goals and a known end, such as a redesign or a first release. Ongoing work at a fixed monthly fee suits a product that keeps changing, because the roadmap can shift without a new contract. Both are priced before work starts.

### How much does it cost to outsource UX design?

The cost depends on the scope and the team, and it is always agreed before work starts. A pilot project has a fixed price agreed up front, a defined project is priced on its agreed scope, and ongoing work is a fixed monthly fee. Your proposal states the figure.

### How do you handle confidential information, NDAs and research data?

Send your security or supplier questionnaire when you receive our proposal, and we answer it before work starts. We sign your NDA and your data processing agreement, and research recordings and notes stay in tools we agree with you. If you need an NDA before you share anything sensitive, say so when you get in touch.

### Does Tcules offer UX consulting services?

Yes. A senior designer can advise your own team: reviewing a flow, testing a direction with users or ranking what to fix through an [audit](/audits). Advice and hands-on design work can sit in the same engagement, priced the same way.

### Can an outsourced design team work with our in-house designers?

Yes. We work in your design files, your design system and your review rhythm, and your designers see our work as it happens. Specialists such as a researcher or a design-systems specialist join when the work calls for them.
