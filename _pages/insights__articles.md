## Product design articles FAQs {#faq}

### Where can I find articles on SaaS product design?

Every piece on this page is written for people building SaaS or business software. Start with CPQ UX design if you make quoting tools, the design system pieces if your library is slipping, and the generative AI patterns if you are planning an AI feature. The table above matches each question to a piece.

### Which articles explain AI trust in interfaces?

Two pieces cover it from different sides. Trust and transparency in AI interfaces sets out what an AI feature should show, and how review should grow with the stakes. AI confidence scores in UX looks at the review step itself, and why a score alone leaves usage flat.

### Are there interviews with product leaders on UX?

Yes. My Experiments with UX is our interview series with people who ship products. Karthik Pasupathy talks about context, clarity and core functionality, and Aishwarya Ashok about building an AI-first product experience. For recorded conversations, see the podcasts.

### What has Tcules written about CPQ UX?

CPQ UX design covers configuration rules that explain themselves, prices and approvals visible before submission, and revisions that keep earlier work. It ends with a checklist. The [Auxentios case study](/case-studies/auxentios-cpq-product-mvp-design) shows our design work on a CPQ product from interviews to MVP.

### When should I read an article instead of a guide?

Read an article when you want a position argued, such as why a confidence score leaves trust unchanged. Reach for a guide when you need to carry out a job step by step, such as onboarding or a UX audit. Many readers use both, argument first and method second.
