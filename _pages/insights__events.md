# Tcules product design events: an Ahmedabad meetup and a filmmaking talk

Talks and meetups we have hosted for designers, engineers and product people, written up so the useful parts reach people who could not attend.

## Past Tcules events, including a design systems meetup in Ahmedabad {#sessions}

**Design systems for everyone, from basics to advanced** (March 2023). Our Ahmedabad meetup, introduced as the city's first on design systems, ran from auto-layout and design tokens to composable systems, with a hands-on exercise. Our own [design systems](/services/design-systems) work covers the same ground for client products.

**When a product designer moves to Bollywood** (2 December 2024). Arastu, who moved from product design into filmmaking in Mumbai, showed how a film travels from idea to audience. Participants tried a group filmmaking exercise.

## Questions about Tcules design events {#faq}

### When did Tcules host a design systems meetup in Ahmedabad?

In March 2023. Tcules hosted it in Ahmedabad, introduced as the city's first meetup on design systems. It covered design systems from the basics to composable systems and included a hands-on exercise.

### Does Tcules run design events?

Yes. Tcules has hosted talks and meetups for designers, engineers and product people, and has written them up so the useful parts reach people who could not attend. The sessions so far are the design systems meetup and a talk on what product designers can learn from filmmaking.

### What was the Tcules talk on product design and filmmaking?

Arastu, a product designer who moved into filmmaking in Mumbai, walked through how a film travels from idea to audience. The session ran on 2 December 2024, and participants also tried a group filmmaking exercise.

Send us a note through the [contact page](/contact) and say whether you want to speak at a session or host one with us. Tell us the topic and who you would like to reach.
