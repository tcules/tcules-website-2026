# Product design insights, sorted by the problem you are working on

Product design insights from the Tcules team: what we have learned designing software products, and where we think they are heading. Start with the problem in front of you, whether a new product, AI inside a product, a design system, an ageing product or a SaaS workflow. Or browse everything by format.

## Building something new: from idea or AI prototype to first release {#new-product}

For founders and product teams taking an idea, or an app built with AI tools, towards a first release.

**[Taking a vibe-coded app to production: what to check, in order](/insights/vibe-coded-app-production-ready).** What an app built with Lovable, Bolt or Cursor must answer before real users rely on it, and the steps from prototype to production.

**[How to choose a product design agency and test the fit](/insights/guides/how-to-choose-a-product-design-agency).** What to check before you hire, and how a studio compares with a freelancer, an in-house hire or a development company.

Our related service: [Design-led MVP Development](/services/mvp-development).

## AI product UX articles for teams adding AI to their software {#ai-product-ux}

For teams adding generated answers, recommendations or automated actions to software that people rely on at work.

**[Trust and transparency in AI interfaces: what to show, and how much](/insights/building-trust-and-transparency-in-ai-first-user-interfaces).** Five things an AI feature should show the person using it, and why a risky action deserves more review than a routine one.

**[Seven generative AI product patterns for SaaS and the controls each needs](/insights/generative-ai-product-patterns-for-saas).** Where AI can work inside a SaaS workflow, from drafting something for a person to judge to acting on their behalf.

Our related service: [AI Product UX](/services/ai-product-ux).

## Design systems that product teams keep using {#design-systems}

For teams building a design system, or wondering why product teams keep working around the one they have.

**[How to build a SaaS design system product teams keep using](/insights/design-systems-for-saas-boosting-efficiency-and-ux-consistency).** One test for any design system. A team needs something the library lacks: does the request end in a shared release or another local copy?

**[Design systems for everyone, from basics to advanced](/insights/events).** Our Ahmedabad meetup on design systems, from auto-layout and design tokens to composable systems.

Our related service: [Design Systems](/services/design-systems).

## Modernising an established product: audits and release quality {#modernisation}

For teams whose product has become hard to change after years of releases, and who need to know what to fix first.

**[How to use a UX audit: from first question to ranked plan](/insights/guides/how-to-use-a-ux-product-audit).** How to run or commission an audit whose findings your team can own, prioritise and check afterwards.

**[Silent UI regressions: how to catch them before customers lose confidence](/insights/silent-ui-regression-debt-erodes-adoption-before-it-shows).** The small interface slips that get past release checks: retried saves, double-checked results and a product that feels a little less dependable with each release.

Our related service: [Product Modernisation](/services/product-modernisation).

## SaaS product design: quoting, engagement and mobile {#saas-ux}

For SaaS teams working on quoting, engagement and where their product should run.

**[CPQ UX design: quoting software sales reps choose over spreadsheets](/insights/cpq-ux-design).** Design quoting software around the quote that several roles contribute to, then judge it by whether reps prefer it to their spreadsheet.

**[SaaS user engagement: measure the behaviour that shows customers getting value](/insights/guides/saas-engagement-and-subscription-decisions).** Why fewer sessions can mean a better product, and which repeated behaviour shows customers are getting value.

**[Does my SaaS need a mobile app? A moment-by-moment test](/insights/guides/saas-does-your-app-need-a-mobile-version).** The answer lies in the work that happens away from a desk and cannot wait, and in whether a phone improves it.

Our related expertise: [B2B SaaS and workflow software](/expertise/b2b-saas-workflow-software).

## Product design insights FAQs {#faq}

### Where can I read practical product design advice for SaaS?

The guides are the most practical place to start: onboarding, user engagement, mobile apps, UX audits and choosing a design partner, each with a method and a worked example. The SaaS section above picks out the pieces on quoting software, engagement and mobile.

### Does Tcules publish articles on AI product UX?

Yes. The AI section on this page collects them: what an AI feature should show people, where generative AI fits in a SaaS workflow and how to design a review step people control. The articles page adds the related interviews.

### What has Tcules written about design systems?

Two articles: one on building a SaaS design system that product teams keep using, and one on when to hire a design system consultant and how to judge one. There is also a write-up of our Ahmedabad meetup on design systems, under events.

### Are there product design podcasts from Tcules?

Yes, two episodes so far. One is with Eden AI's CPTO on building AI around the work users need done; the other is a panel of product, engineering and design leaders on making product decisions together. Each comes with a written summary.

### Who writes Tcules insights?

The Tcules team writes them, and every piece carries its author's name and the date it was last updated. The authors page lists each writer with everything they have published.
