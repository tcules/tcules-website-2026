# Shoghi Bagul, Product Designer at Tcules

Product Designer, Tcules

Shoghi Bagul, a Product Designer at Tcules, writes about AI product UX, generative AI patterns for SaaS and design systems.

One piece sets out seven generative AI patterns, from a draft a person judges to a task the AI carries out for them. Another looks at how much an AI feature should show about its role, sources and limits as the stakes rise.

The design system article follows a product team's request for a new component state through to a shared release.
