Our design engineering services put your product's behaviour in code early, so problems surface while they are cheap to fix and releases match the design.

## What you get from our design engineering services {#what-you-get}

Our design engineers take one interaction, screen or component from an idea to working code, inside your product or beside it.

- **A coded prototype** of the interaction in question, with real content and the awkward cases, tried in the browser. [Coded Prototyping](/services/design-engineering/coded-prototyping)

- **Behaviour your developers can build from:** a spec, a working example or code taken from your Figma designs. [Design to Code](/services/design-engineering/design-to-code)

- **Tables, editors and drag and drop, built to hold up,** with keyboard use and every state. [Frontend Design Systems](/services/design-engineering/frontend-design-systems)

- **A check of what shipped** against the agreed behaviour, on real data and small screens, with the gaps fixed. [Implementation QA](/services/design-engineering/implementation-qa)

- **Production code** that your engineers review, or the whole build through [Software Development](/services/software-development).

**Who does the work.** Design engineers, who design and code, do the work, led by a senior product designer, with an account lead running the schedule.

**What we need from you.** The interaction in question, a sandbox or access to your codebase, and realistic data, including the awkward cases.

**Where our part stops.** Your engineers review every change before it joins your codebase, and you choose the direction. A full product build is Software Development, and your platform and release process stay yours.

## How we test an interaction in code before it is built {#method}

1. **Name the question:** one interaction or screen, and what your team needs to see before committing to it.

2. **Build it in code,** in a sandbox or inside your codebase, with realistic data and the awkward cases included.

3. **Review it together.** Your designers, developers and product owner try it in the browser and choose a direction.

4. **Carry it into the product** as a buildable spec, a working example or production code.

5. **Check what shipped** against the agreed behaviour.

*Illustration, not client work*

A team-planning product wants people to drag work between weeks and see who is overloaded while they drag. Nobody can judge how that feels from a static design, so we would build it in code with realistic data.

We would try three ways of showing overload while an item moves. Each would then meet the awkward cases: mouse, trackpad and keyboard; two people moving the same item; a week holding hundreds of items.

By the end we would know which version people read at a glance, and what the product does when a move breaks a rule.

## Design engineering vs frontend development and design systems {#compare}

Pick design engineering for one interaction that must feel right; the others cover shared components, a full build or testing with users.

| Service | What you get | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Design engineering** | Product behaviour designed and tried in code, then carried into your product | A feature or interaction that has to feel right before and after it ships |
| **[Design Systems](/services/design-systems)** | One shared set of components, rules and ownership across products | Several teams or products that need to look and behave as one |
| **[Frontend Development](/services/software-development/frontend-development)** | The full interface of a product, built to an agreed design | A larger build where the design questions are already answered |
| **[Prototyping & Usability Testing](/services/product-design/prototyping-usability-testing)** | Clickable prototypes tested with users | Questions about layout, flow and wording |

## Questions about design engineering services {#faq}

### Is design engineering the same as frontend development?

They share the code and serve different needs. Design engineering at Tcules explores, refines and builds product behaviour in code, beside your developers. [Frontend Development](/services/software-development/frontend-development) covers the wider build of a larger product.

### Can design engineers work in our codebase?

Yes. Tcules design engineers prototype, build or review inside your own code, alongside your engineers and following your review rules. Your engineers check each change before it goes in, as they would for their own team.

### Do you use AI coding tools in design engineering?

Yes. Tcules uses AI coding tools for well-defined tasks, so a team can compare several working directions where it once compared two pictures. People choose the direction and review the code, and release-bound work goes through the testing agreed for the project.

### Can we hire a design engineer for one project?

Yes. Tcules takes on design engineering as a defined project, such as one prototype or one tricky component, or as part of ongoing product work. Each shape is priced up front once the scope is agreed, and [How We Work](/how-we-work) explains the options.

### Who offers design engineering services for SaaS?

Specialist studios, freelance design engineers and some product teams' own staff. Tcules offers it inside a product design studio, so the people who shape the experience also take it into code, as the Benchmark Gensuite and ConnectX cases show.
