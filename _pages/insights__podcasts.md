Two recorded episodes with product, engineering and design leaders. Each episode page sums up the main points in writing and links to the full recording on YouTube, so you can read first and listen to the parts that matter to you.

## Product design podcast episodes {#episodes}

**[Eden AI's Samy Melaine on building AI products for users: podcast](/insights/podcasts/do-ai-not-for-ai-s-sake-but-for-your-user-insights-from-eden-ai-s-ctpo).** Eden AI's CPTO on choosing models, using the roadmap to communicate, and how a developer platform stays clear for people new to AI.

**[Product design decisions podcast: how product, engineering and design leaders agree](/insights/podcasts/mastering-design-decisions).** A panel with Medkart's founder, Contrado's head of engineering and the creative head of Knovos. Watch the recordings on YouTube: [Eden AI's CPTO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNz1zr3U7Zo) and [the product decisions panel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=44Nta0CkcOY).

## Product design podcast FAQs {#faq}

### Is there a podcast on building AI products for users?

Yes. Samy Melaine, Founder and CPTO of Eden AI, talks about choosing models, using the roadmap to communicate and keeping a developer platform clear for people new to AI. The written summary covers the main points.

### Which podcast episode discusses product design decisions?

The panel with leaders from Medkart, Contrado and Knovos. They cover listening to customers, reading data, roadmap trade-offs and how teams handle disagreement. The episode page lists the takeaways before the recording.

### Does Tcules host a podcast?

Yes. Tcules hosts recorded conversations with people who build software, and publishes each one with a written summary and a link to the full recording on YouTube. Two episodes are out so far.

### Where can I hear product leaders discuss design trade-offs?

Start with the panel episode, where a founder, a head of engineering and a creative head talk through roadmap compromises and disagreement between teams. For written interviews on similar themes, our My Experiments with UX series sits with the articles.
