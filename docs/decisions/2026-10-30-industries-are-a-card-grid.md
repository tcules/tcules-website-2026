"Industries we know well" renders as the standard 3-column card grid with each sector's photo (16:9, hairline, radius 8)
and its name, the same card shape as every other grid.

**Why.** A list of names with thumbnails at varying sizes on the far edge was a one-off shape for a common job.

**The mistake it prevents.** A bespoke shape per band for a job the grid already does.

**How to apply.** IndustriesBand uses the 3-column card grid on every page.
