Clients' words live in a page's quotes band only. A quote the copy sets under a claim is cut (logged in
docs/content-changes.md), never moved into the claim's band as a small testimonial. Every band renders
through one shape the home, service or expertise pages already use, never a merge of two: where-to-start
cards, the Why Tcules glyph list (OfferSheet `list`), the three-ways glyph cards (EngagementOptions
`cards`), the ink CTA island, the FAQ's halves. Lines that fight the shape are cut and logged.
**Why.** Quotes inside claims and mixed layouts made both pages read as assembled rather than designed.
**The mistake it prevents.** A testimonial card wedged under a claim; a band that mixes two components.
**How to apply.** plan `SITUATION_PROSE`, `CLAIM_LIST`, `CARD_SETS`; CopyBands TextBand.
