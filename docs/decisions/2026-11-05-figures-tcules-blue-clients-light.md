Illustrations carry people. A **blue** figure is a Tcules person; a **white or light-blue** figure is a client (or
their user). The arm is the home hero's: one stroke from inside the shoulder, a round cap and a round hand, about a
quarter of the body's width. A Tcules arm is solid blue with no outline ("the hand of the blue figure has an
outline and may read like a stick"); a client's arm and hand are light, outlined in blue. Both are the same overall
weight in one drawing. A Tcules figure is edged in white so it stays separate from blue objects it overlaps. A scene
never holds four blue figures: where it shows how Tcules works with clients, it pairs one Tcules figure with one
client figure.

**Why.** The pictures need "whose world" (30 Oct), and once people are in, who they are has to read at a glance;
a stick-like arm, a heavier client arm or a blue figure merging into a blue screen each broke that on the drawn set.

**The mistake it prevents.** The first `/why-tcules` band, with four blue figures; the approved heroes' white-cased
stick arms; client arms drawn solid blue (AI Product UX) or thicker than the Tcules arm beside them.

**How to apply.** STYLE.md §5. Applied to the ten approved heroes and every drawing of the 5 Nov run.
