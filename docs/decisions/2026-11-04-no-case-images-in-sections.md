A band that argues a claim does not show a client project's screenshot or cover beside its words, even
when the copy binds one (the Benchmark Gensuite prototype, the ConnectX demo). Case imagery belongs to
case cards and case pages. The band's claims take their own shape instead: two to four short titled
claims are the three-ways cards, each led by its glyph (EngagementOptions `cards`; plan `CARD_SETS`).
**Why.** The screenshot read as a case card dropped into an argument, and set the claims as a column of
large titles beside it.
**The mistake it prevents.** A project's image standing in for a section's visual.
**How to apply.** Leave a bound case image unused; content-changes records the unbinding.
