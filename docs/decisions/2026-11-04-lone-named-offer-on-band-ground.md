A band that names one offer and links it is that offer's wide ServiceTile (name, the copy's sentence as its `body`, its own drawing, the action for what it opens), never one ruled row beside the head. Alone on its band it takes `frame="none"`: no hairline and no inset, the words on the band's ground and the drawing beside them; it still lifts on hover and no edge appears on hover. It extends 2026-10-29-named-services-are-service-tiles to a set of one.

**Why.** Directly under a boxed chooser, a second boxed full-width tile read as a stray sixth tile in another dialect; separating by arrangement, not by a second box, keeps the two offers apart.

**The mistake it prevents.** Head beside one ruled row; two boxed full-width tiles of different construction 100px apart.

**How to apply.** CopyBands RelatedBand (single named offer); Bento ServiceTile `body`, `frame="none"` (specimens 9b, 9d).
