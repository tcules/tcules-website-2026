The repeated "Three ways to work with us" band is a pricing table without prices: EngagementOptions
`plans`, three equal cards a `gutter` apart, each a hairline card on white with its name and, a
`tight` step under it, one line saying what the plan is for, both over a hairline (no label line
above the name: owner, 5 Nov 2026, final copy), then its checklist with one shared blue tick on every
line, then its own action at the foot: a full-width blue outline Button at `md` (owner: "one size smaller"), the same on all three, so no
card is featured. Names drop their article ("Pilot project"). The name is the new `--text-h4-lg`
(26 / 24 / 22, sans). No glyph, no eyebrow, no "Yours" or "Working hours" terms. The
"Not sure which suits you?" line and its links stay the compact ink island a `section` under the set.
Every page carrying the band carries the same copy.

**Why.** "Design this like a pricing component without literal prices. Drop eyebrows. Description
to be replaced by checklist … drop 'yours' 'working hours'"; then on the mocks: "replace the icons
with eyebrow", "make all the buttons outline blue", "slightly larger titles", "remove articles from
the titles", and of the two list treatments "go with E" (all icons the same icon).

**The mistake it prevents.** The 4 Nov cards (glyph, name, a sentence each, terms under the set) and
mock D, a glyph per checklist line that every authored item would have had to pick.

**How to apply.** Author the band as bold name with its line after it, bullet list, one link, per option, then the
closing line and its links (`_pages/*` `#how-we-work-together`); `planOptions` reads that shape and
falls back to the older readings for anything else. The final copy (names, lines, checklists,
button labels) landed 5 Nov 2026 on all 49 pages. /how-we-work's `#engagement-shapes` is a different band and keeps
its own form.
