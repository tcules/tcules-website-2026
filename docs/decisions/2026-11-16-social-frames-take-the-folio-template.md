Every carousel, single image and story is built on one template, the Folio direction (A). Paper
carries the set. The turn (frame 04) is on ink, and the close is on blue with a still of the texture
field. A frame carries no eyebrow, no running title and no byline: one serif heading, then sans. Every
frame ends in a foot: a hairline, the wordmark at the start and the slot at the end, which holds the
swipe arrow on the cover and `NN / NN` on every other frame. The cover is headed by the wordmark
instead and has an isometric illustration drawn for its title. The close's button is the site's solid
button on blue. A frame may carry one peridot mark.

**Why.** The owner picked A over B ("maybe") and C ("no") on the canvas, then reviewed it frame by
frame on real copy (Yash's LinkedIn week of 17 Nov 2026). The eyebrow named a content type the
heading already makes plain, so it was dropped.

**The mistake it prevents.** Adding back eyebrows ("POINT OF VIEW · MVP SCOPE"), drawing chips, a blue
frame 04 or capitals labels because a content file asks for them. The week's file asked for all four,
and the canvas rulings overrode them.

**How to apply.** The template lives in the `tcules-social` folder, not in this repo (see
2026-11-24-social-posts-live-outside-the-website-repo). Chapter 10 still describes eyebrows and
families. Update it from this entry in the next `/dbf-update ch10`.
