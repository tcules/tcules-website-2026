Every cover, including a text post's single image, gets a new isometric illustration in the house style,
drawn for that post's title. The idea is agreed in words before anything is drawn. An inner frame may
use any existing flat Sketch scene that fits what the frame says, or a new flat drawing when none
does (product-promise frame 06 got `first-run-five-screens`).

**Why.** Website hero illustrations borrowed for covers were close to the topic but not to the post's
line.

**The mistake it prevents.** Reusing a hero illustration because its subject matches by keyword. A
drawing that counts things must show the number the frame says: the first five-screens drawing showed
six.

**How to apply.** `tcules-social` keeps each week's concept notes and generators beside the drawings.
Chapter 5 has no placement spec for social covers yet.
