In an industries band, a link to the industries hub ("See all industries →") is the band's end action in
the head. Only sector links become cards.

**Why.** The link leads to the hub, not to a sector; as a card it took a placeholder photograph and read as a
ninth industry.

**The mistake it prevents.** The 3 Nov preview: "See all industries →" as the last card, on an "Image to come"
plate, on home and every offer page.

**How to apply.** Any band of destination cards: a link to the set's own hub is the head's action.
