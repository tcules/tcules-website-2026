Tag `variant="link"` pills (sentence case, hairline edge, round ends, 44 tall) in one wrapping row. No
photographs.
**Why.** "Short band like so for industries we know well", carried to every page with the band.
**The mistake it prevents.** A fold of photo cards for a list of links.
**How to apply.** CopyBands `IndustriesBand`; kinds.ts `industries` → `C-pills`.
