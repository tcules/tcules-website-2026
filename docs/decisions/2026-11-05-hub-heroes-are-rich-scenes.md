A hub's hero (`/services`) and a band-wide drawing on an important page (`/why-tcules`) show several aspects in one
screen, organised on one base around one hero object, the way the expertise hub drawing puts six industries on one
app: roughly six to twelve parts, each simple, the whole still reading in three seconds. An item-scale or a single
page's drawing stays minimal.

**Why.** A hub stands for a whole line of work; one small idea undersells it.

**The mistake it prevents.** The `/services` pilot: a laptop, three figures and a sketch on the floor.

**How to apply.** STYLE.md §1. The `/services` hero (one bench, the whole service line) and the `/why-tcules` band.
