A service page asks twice: the ink Island "Tell us what you're working on" that closes How we work together
(one button to the contact page), and the enquiry form Island at the end of the page.

**Why.** They catch two different readers: one ready after reading how an engagement works, one who reads
to the end. The owner kept both when asked.

**The mistake it prevents.** A critic or a later pass dropping one of the two as "asking twice".

**How to apply.** Keep both on every L1 service page. They must never sit adjacent; a band always separates them.
