Where a band shows the studio itself, its heading and words sit in the top-left corner with six real
studio photographs round them in an L. One large frame sits beside the copy, at least 3:2 and growing with
the copy. Under both runs a row of three equal thirds: tall frames at the two ends, and in the middle a 3:2
frame over two 3:4 ones. The photographs sit `--gap-text` (16) apart on both axes. The copy and the large
frame are a split (`section`, 96).

**Why.** The owner wanted the people and the place seen as a set, not one photo beside a paragraph. At the
card seam (32) the six read as separate pictures; at 16 they read as one collage.

**The mistake it prevents.** A uniform grid of equal photos, which repeats one shape down the band. Also an
L whose copy column runs down into the photo rows and leaves a hole under the words.

**How to apply.** Use `Collage` for a band about the studio and its people. Pick each tile's photo, focal
point and zoom by eye, as a placement choice, and never put them on the catalogue record. `zoom` is
Media's: it applies only to a cropped photograph in a box that clips it.
