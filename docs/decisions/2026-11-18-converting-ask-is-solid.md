"Book a free product review" is always the primary solid button, in a band's head, foot or strip as well as in the hero; on ink and blue it is the surface's solid (paper, per the 30 Oct ink-solid ruling). Every other in-band action stays a link.

**Why.** Owner: "Book a free product review is a primary button." The conversion is one action, and rendering it as a link in a band made it read as a way on rather than the ask.

**The mistake it prevents.** The ask set as a mono link under the "Your demo works" rows.

**How to apply.** Mark the action `converts` (CtaRow `converts`, the overhaul's `BOOK_REVIEW`); never restyle one instance.
