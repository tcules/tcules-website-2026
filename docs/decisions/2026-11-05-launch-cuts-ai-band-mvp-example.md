Home no longer carries "How our designers use AI" ("kill section"). /services/mvp-development no longer
carries its worked example, the "Illustration, not client work" vet-estimate scenario after "What an MVP
should include" ("It means nothing here"); its lock drawing stays. Every other page keeps its worked
example.

**Why.** In the review the AI run was "just an extra information", with no win or loss riding on it, and
Jatin cut it. On the MVP page the example floats on its own rather than sitting in a section as it does
elsewhere.

**The mistake it prevents.** Re-adding the AI band to home, or removing the worked example from every
page because it left the MVP page.

**How to apply.** The bands are gone from `_pages/home.md` and `_pages/services__mvp-development.md`; the
CMS entries for those pages must leave them out too.
