Home's "Where is your product today?" is variant H of the comparison round
(https://claude.ai/artifact/1Bxey8Wddj5soohGeUT5fu): centred head on tint; three paper cards, each its
blue label, title and description, a `stack` (48) of air, then its two ways in as ruled rows in ink;
no examples, in the cards or under them. The set closes a `stack` (48) under the cards on one centred
row: a line for whoever sits between the doors ("Somewhere in between? …", placeholder copy) at the
lead role, then the band's ask ("Book a call") as a primary solid, size `md`, the owner exception to
2026-10-07-blue-solid-is-the-one-main-action carried over from the entry this replaces. Below 768 the
line stacks over the button, both centred.
**Why.** The examples are removed from the section completely; the case studies band below carries the
work. Alone under the cards the button "feels orphaned", so a line gives it its reason.
**The mistake it prevents.** Putting a case line back under each door.
**How to apply.** The copy (`_pages/home.md`) authors no "Example:" line in the band; GatewayPaths
`form="cards"` renders no example row when a path has none.
