Home's Why Tcules renders through the plan's Reasons like every other page: head above, each reason its
illustration (the labelled slot until drawn) above its title and sentence. It would supersede
2026-10-30-home-reasons-beside-words-only once the owner promotes it.

**Why.** The 3 Nov copy gives home the same six reasons as the offer pages, so one drawn set serves both, and
six words-only rows beside a short head read as a long text column.

**The mistake it prevents.** Home's six reasons as a words-only column beside the heading.

**How to apply.** HomePage no longer overrides `why-tcules`; the drawings for the six reasons are one set
for home and the offer pages (`getReasonFigure`, keyed by title).
