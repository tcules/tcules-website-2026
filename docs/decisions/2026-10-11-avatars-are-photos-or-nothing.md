Avatars are circular photos or nothing; initials in a box are banned.

**Why.** A box of initials is a placeholder pretending to be a person.

**The mistake it prevents.** Initials avatars in testimonial cards.

**How to apply.** TestimonialCard, ProfileCard. ch05 §6 says "No photograph is masked to a circle", which this contradicts for avatars.
