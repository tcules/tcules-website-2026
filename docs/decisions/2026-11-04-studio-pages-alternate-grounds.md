From the first band after the client marks to the quotes band, the bands take tint and paper in turn,
counted back from the quotes band (tint), so two tints never touch. The enquiry form and the FAQ stay paper.
**Why.** Rhythm on two pages of mostly text sets.
**The mistake it prevents.** A long run of paper bands that read as one.
**How to apply.** plan.ts `ALTERNATING`; each band's renderer takes the unit's surface.
