A heading straight over its own words, with no lead between, is head to body: a `block` (24), not the block-to-block `stack` (48). A head that ends in a lead keeps lead → body at 48. The same holds for a logo wall whose head is only its eyebrow: the label sits 24 over the wall.

**Why.** "Spacing between title and body looks off" (/about, "The Ahmedabad studio", "How AI is changing our practice"); the logo wall's eyebrow was "too far from the grid".

**The mistake it prevents.** At 48 a title floated free of the paragraphs it heads, and the logo wall's label read as belonging to nothing.

**How to apply.** ContentSection's media split (copy beside a photo or drawing), unless the body is pinned to the photo's foot; LogoGrid when the head has no heading and no action.
