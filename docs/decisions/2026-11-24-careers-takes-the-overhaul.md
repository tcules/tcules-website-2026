/careers follows the overhaul: the hero carries the studio stats; the open roles come first on tint, led by
their count ("4 roles", counted from the active jobs) and without per-department icons; "Product designer
jobs in Ahmedabad…" is the words beside one studio photograph, not the three-photo collage; "The work…"
and "How AI fits into the job" sit beside their headings, the AI band with "See how we use AI" under it;
"What we look for…" is two columns of checks under its heading; the "No matching role" line is a note on a
hairline under the application's lead. Every band keeps its anchor.

**Why.** The overhaul is the final design; the owner chose its version of each band that differed.

**The mistake it prevents.** A template that sets every band to paper in code, so the CMS ground never
reaches the page.

**How to apply.** Careers bands take their ground from the CMS; an editorial band set "media" puts its
words beside its photograph, "head beside" beside its heading with the link block as its one link.
