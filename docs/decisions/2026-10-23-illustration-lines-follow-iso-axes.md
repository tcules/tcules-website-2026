"While the shapes are drawn on the isometric plane, connecting lines etc are never drawn on the grids. i want there to be a 3D isometric grid so any line that has to go from A to B has to follow an isometric path." Connectors, guides, threads, leaders and in-face marks run along x, y or z and turn only at corners.

**Why.** A free diagonal breaks the drawing's 3D grid and reads as a 2D overlay.

**The mistake it prevents.** The first enquiry draft's wireframe "X" placeholder, whose diagonals cut across the panel.

**How to apply.** Every illustration generator. ch05 §4a states this for the carried item's path only; it widens to every line at the next /dbf-update.
