/how-we-work follows the overhaul's band order and copy: Stages, "The engagement moves with your product"
(the drawing across the band, the six points as tiles under it), plans, "What stays the same" (four text
tiles), What clients say (Home's six), "How we use AI in our own work", the terms, the enquiry, the FAQ and
the free tools. "Who you work with" and "From design to working software" leave the page.

**The AI band is the overhaul's two paragraphs beside the heading**, with "See how we use AI" under them,
not the live icon rows.

**The terms keep their drawings** with the overhaul's copy. The overhaul set them as text-only tiles,
which tracker O.15 would have had to give a shape later.

**Why.** The overhaul is the final copy and order; the terms already had the shape O.15 asks for.

**The mistake it prevents.** Porting a text-only set over drawings that already answer O.15, then
redesigning it again.

**How to apply.** A How we work band with a section drawing and entries is the drawn facts; an editorial
band set "head beside" puts its words beside the heading with its link block as the band's one link.
