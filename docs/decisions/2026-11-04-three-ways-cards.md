The repeated "Three ways to work with us" band is EngagementOptions `cards` on every page (49): three
separate bordered cards a `gutter` apart, each led by its glyph (flag, clipboard-check, refresh), then
its label line where the copy has one, name and words; the terms a `gutter` under the cards with no
hairline; the closing ask a `section` (96) below. Where the copy closes on a line of its own ("Not sure
which suits you?") the ask is the compact ink island (CtaSection `density="compact"`, in band): words
at the start, actions at the end. No scope drawings in this band.
**Why.** "These also need to be cards that look like cards"; "icons or small graphics"; "make the
boxes disjointed"; "remove the hairline … so the hierarchy is clear".
**The mistake it prevents.** Borderless drawn tiles that read as a gallery, or a panel of joined cells.
**How to apply.** OfferBands `tiles`, `OPTION_GLYPHS`; CtaSection compact.
