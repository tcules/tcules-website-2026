A bento tile's drawing is its service's own Sketch scene, cropped to its drawn edge (no plate) and in the light strokes. "Light stroke on the graphics." The six isometric service illustrations were shown in the same layout and not chosen.

**Why.** The flat scenes read differently from the Gateway's isometric drawings two bands above; at the kit's weight they read heavy in a small tile.

**The mistake it prevents.** Sketch scenes on their dot-field plate inside a tile (a box in a box, its margin reading as an indent), and the kit's 2 / 3 strokes at tile size.

**How to apply.** Sketch `frame="bare"` and `weight="light"` (one step down the border ramp: 1 / 2 / 2). The kit weight stays the default everywhere else.
