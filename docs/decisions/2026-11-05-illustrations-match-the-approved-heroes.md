The standard for an isometric illustration is the set of approved hero drawings in `public/illustrations/heroes/`:
the dotted floor diamond, one solid blue hero object with a thick edge, blue outlines on white with brandeis tints for
side faces, one peridot accent, rounded corners, text as bars. Real-life objects are welcome (a house, a wind turbine,
a card terminal, a desk). The older drawing rules are guidance where they agree with those drawings, not constraints
where they don't: a line may run on any angle, a hero may be wide, and a drawing may face either way.

**Why.** The heroes Jatin and Bhumika froze on 30 Oct already broke several older rules and were approved as they
are. Holding new drawings to the older rules would make them a different hand from the heroes beside them.

**The mistake it prevents.** A new drawing routed on the iso axes, squeezed into 0.75–1.35, or kept abstract and
object-free because ch05 §4a said so, so it reads as another studio's work next to the approved heroes.

**How to apply.** `docs/illustrations/run-2026-11-05/STYLE.md` writes the hand out (palette, weights, floor, people,
devices, sizes). Judge a new drawing beside the approved heroes, not against ch05 alone.
