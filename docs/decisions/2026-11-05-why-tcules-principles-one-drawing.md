The `/why-tcules` band "Better product decisions, before you build" shows ONE drawing at the content width between
its head and its three principles: a studio table where three pairs, each one Tcules person and one client person,
work on one principle each. The principles under it are words only. Other reason sets keep one illustration per
reason (2026-10-30-reasons-take-illustrations).

client; one scene shows all three principles as one way of working.
