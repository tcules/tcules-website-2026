A table of options a reader chooses between (a router) is the /services hub's split tiles from two rows, not three. A split tile left alone on the last row of four tracks takes the whole row from 1024: its drawing beside its words in the named half, the panel the last third. Never a split tile beside a hole.

**Why.** The two-row pages fell back to ruled rows while their siblings had tiles, and the /audits chooser's fifth tile sat beside an empty half row.

**The mistake it prevents.** A ruled router on some audit pages and tiles on others; a lone tile leaving half a row empty (also on mvp-development, product-redesign, saas-web-app-development and figma-storybook-alignment, which this fills).

**How to apply.** OfferBands Router (option tables from two rows); Bento full-row odd split tile (specimen 2d3); /audits `choose` is `router` in plan.ts.
