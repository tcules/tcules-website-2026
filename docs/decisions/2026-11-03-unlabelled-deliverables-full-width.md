Where the copy authors no label over the deliverables (the 3 Nov copy dropped "What you receive"), the drawn
deliverables sit full width under the band's head, their titles as h3s, and the three terms (who does the
work, what we need, where our part stops) stay rows in their label column below. A label, when authored,
keeps the label row of 2026-10-30-what-you-get-label-rows.

**Why.** With no label the label column has nothing to hold; the deliverables are the band's object and
take the width, while the terms keep the rows the owner asked for.

**The mistake it prevents.** The 3 Nov preview: an empty label column leaving a blank quarter beside five
squeezed deliverable cards.

**How to apply.** OfferParts on every What you get band; the label row returns as soon as the copy authors
a label.
