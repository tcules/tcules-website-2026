In an illustration, a laptop or a monitor is drawn with a light body (white faces, brandeis-100/200 sides, a blue
outline) and only its screen in dark blue. A phone may stay solid blue.

**Why.** A fully blue laptop is one heavy mass; the light body keeps the screen, where the work is, as the object
that carries the colour.

**The mistake it prevents.** The `/services` pilot's solid blue laptop.

**How to apply.** STYLE.md §3. The `/services` hero, the `/why-tcules` band and the designer's desk follow it.
