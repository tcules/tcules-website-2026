The three service pages follow the overhaul's band order and copy: Stages, the first-call chat thread,
Selected work as Home's wide alternating cards, Why choose Tcules, the testimonial wall, plans, the
enquiry, the FAQ, the insights, the free tools and the onward links. MVP Development adds "Your demo
works. Launch needs what sits underneath." (ink) and "Your prototype is the brief we build from" (the
keep / fix / rebuild legend), both rows beside their drawing.

**Why choose Tcules keeps the live list.** Each page takes the overhaul's own rows (and Home its own),
drawn in the live icon list, not the overhaul's hanging rows (owner, 23 Nov 2026: "i like how the list
looks on the live website now"). A row's icon is named in the CMS.

**The onward links are the overhaul's ruled rows** with an arrow beside the heading, set per page in the
CMS (Service Card `rows`); a page not yet rebuilt keeps its drawn cards.

**The overhaul's removals stand:** What you get, the problems or keep-and-change tabs, the method,
Industries we know well, the MVP's five choices and its comparison leave these pages.

**A testimonial can link its case** ("Read the Doodle case"), on Home and every wall.

**Why.** The overhaul is final for these pages, and one band per job keeps them in step with Home.

**The mistake it prevents.** Rebuilding a service page in code, or shipping half a page's CMS fields and
deploying the CMS again for each small change.

**How to apply.** CMS changes for a batch ship as one CMS PR, the front end as one PR (and its
production copy), the content as one run, in that order.
