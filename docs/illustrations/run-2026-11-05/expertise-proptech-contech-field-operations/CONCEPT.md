every sign-off", and its title names deal teams, engineers and people on site. Idea 1 is that sentence drawn: it
covers both ends of the page (the acquisitions desk and the field app) where ideas 2 and 3 each cover one item on
the "designs" list. It keeps the hub's house and lime tile, and the hub's blue property card with its map pin, so a
visitor coming from /expertise recognises their field at once.
