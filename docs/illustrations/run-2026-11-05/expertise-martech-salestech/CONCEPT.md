## Fix pass (5 Nov): the easel was redrawn

The critic and the blind reader rejected the report on the easel: the easel is the stock "presentation" symbol and
could sit on any industry page, the reader guessed "AI analytics / a consulting pitch", the board was wall-display
scale, the AI badge plus four rising bars read as a stock slide, and the three pawns had no roles. Fixes applied:

- **New object, read on sight as sales and marketing:** a sales funnel. Leads go in at the top and one finished
  quote comes out at the bottom. It is not a folder (B2B), a flat tablet (commerce, data), a phone rising from an
  object (healthtech), a card catalogue (content) or a desk calendar (marketplaces).
- **The artefact the page designs is on it:** the quote, with a title, three line items with amounts, a rule, and
  a lime total with a tick. AI appears only as the quote's drafter: a small blue sparkle in its corner. No AI badge,
  no bar chart.
- **Roles are marked:** two people, no audience. The customer wears a briefcase and holds the quote; the rep wears
  a target (the marketing and sales mark) and has a hand on the funnel's stand. The front-left and front-right of
  the floor each hold a person.
- **Scale:** the funnel is about 200 × 230 units in a 776-wide box, the size of healthtech's phone, not a wall
  display.
- **It stands:** the funnel sits in the ring of a retort stand: a base plate on the floor, one rod, a level arm to
  the ring's left-most point. The quote hangs from inside the spout (its top is behind the spout's front wall) and
  is held at its edge by the customer, so nothing floats.

## Candidate ideas

1. **The funnel that hands over one clear quote.** Lead cards drop into a sales funnel; out of its spout comes one
   quote with a lime total, and the customer takes it.
2. **The quote in the outbox tray.** A rep lays a ticked quote in an outbox tray, a customer reaching for it.
3. **The pipeline board.** Stage columns of deal cards, one deal in lime, a rep moving it.

**Pick: 1.** The funnel is the one object a buyer names as sales and marketing in a second, and it holds at 360,
where the critic found the easel's content shrank to texture. It says the page's lead literally: what comes out the
end and goes to a customer is clear enough to stand behind. The tray is a desk object with no industry cue (any
office); the pipeline board is a screen of columns, a second wall display, and needs UI to read.
