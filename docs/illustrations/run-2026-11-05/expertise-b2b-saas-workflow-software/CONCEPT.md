## The object

The hub has no object for this industry. The chosen one is a **deal file**: an expanding file with tabbed
dividers, the real-world object for "one record that many roles share". It is drawn the way the hub draws its
objects: a simple isometric volume, solid blue as the hero object, white pages with blue outlines.

## Three ideas (all built around the deal file)

1. **One file, a tab for every role.** A blue expanding file holds one record; each divider is tabbed with a
   role (two people, one AI), and the page being handed on is lifted out with a lime tab.
2. **The file passed across a desk.** An open deal file lies flat on the floor between a client and a Tcules
   designer, one page sliding from one side to the other.
3. **The file on a laptop.** The same tabbed file stands beside a laptop whose screen shows the record with one
   lime "hand on" button.

## Pick: 1

The page says it designs "B2B software that many roles share" and keeps "each record and handover clear".
Idea 1 draws both halves in one object: the tabs are the roles (and the AI agent the lead names), the record
being handed from one person to the next is the clear handover. It reads in three seconds as "a shared file, organised by who does what".
Idea 2 loses the roles; idea 3 repeats healthtech's "object beside a screen" formula.

Against the siblings: healthtech (case left, phone right, three people on a line), fintech (card and phone),
climate (turbine and wall display), edtech (book stairs and phone), proptech (house on lime tile, laptop,
tablet). This is the only one with **no screen**: the product is a printed thing, the record itself. The hero
object stands at the right, the handover (record between two people) at the left.

## Revisions

1. First pass: the lifted page sat in front of the dividers and hid every role tab; people stood apart.
2. Lifted page moved behind the dividers and raised, so the three staggered role tabs (person, person, AI) sit
   in front of its lower half; file made lower and squarer so it reads as a desktop file box.
3. People turned toward the file and moved to its front-left; floor recentred.
4. Floor cut tighter (healthtech's plate: same dots, inset and dashed edge), Tcules figure moved apart from
   the client so the two never overlap.
5. Zoom pass on joins: the pocket face's right corners squared where they meet the gusset (rounded corners
   left a notch), the pocket's top-edge strip stopped short of the rounded front corner, the lime tab drawn
   behind the page instead of over its outline (a blue halo showed round it). Box widened to 1.28:1.

6. Critic and blind-reader pass (5 Nov). The blind reader saw "a desk tray of people records" and figures
   pointing at nothing; the critic asked for the handover to be drawn and to go to someone. Redrawn:
   - **The handover is the picture.** The record is out of the file, carried along the floor's x axis between
     the two people: the Tcules designer (right) passes it, the client (left) receives it, each hand on one
     of its edges. It no longer floats or stands as "the front card"; it is held. Where it came from is drawn
     plainly instead of with drop lines: its slot is empty, marked by the dashed lime outline of the missing
     sheet and its tab, and a dashed arc runs from that empty tab to the record's lime tab.
   - **Lime tab arrow** drawn in the record's own plane, pointing along the x axis toward the client (the way
     the record is going), not screen-horizontal.
   - **A deal record, not a profile.** Small owner avatar and title, a four-step stage stepper (two done in
     blue, the current one lime, one to come) and a value field. Reads as a deal or pipeline record (Dealpath,
     CPQ approvals in the brief), not as a person.
   - **An expanding file, not a desk tray.** The flat right-side triangle is now an accordion gusset: V notches
     along its top, dark valley folds and light ridge folds down to the floor.
   - **Back edge.** The separate board sliver is gone: the back is one shape with its top corners rounded and
     its foot square on the floor.
   - **Pocket label** carries the account: one bold bar and a small count chip. No figure stands near it.
   - **Role tabs** (person, person, AI) now one size and evenly spaced.
   - **Balance.** The handover fills the plate's left half; the plate trimmed (395 × 260) so the file and the
     pair share it with healthtech's air. Box 1.43:1, within 5:4–3:2.

Zoom-checked at 2400px: gusset folds meet the pocket face and floor cleanly; the ghost outline's dashes sit
inside the back board; the arc stops at the board's top edge above the empty tab; the Tcules arm clears its
head (white edge intact against the record); both hands sit on the record's edges at the same size.
Checked at 360: the file with its accordion side, the three role tabs, the record held between two people,
the lime tab and the lime stage, and the arc back to the empty slot all read.

7. Family-check fix (5 Nov). The check found four faults; each fixed at its cause:
   - **The record floated and ran off the plate.** It was a large panel at head height past the floor's left
     edge, so arms reaching it read as pointing at a floating screen. Now it is a sheet the size of the file's
     own dividers, gripped at both ends at hand height, wholly inside the plate. Nothing floats.
   - **Two screen-like surfaces.** The pocket's white card (title bar and count chip) is now a plain label slip
     with one bar. The record is the drawing's one UI surface: owner line and a four-stop stage stepper (the
     current stop ringed in blue, so lime stays on the tab alone).
   - **The dashed arc is gone** (a second idea). Where the record came from is said by the empty slot alone:
     the dashed lime outline of its tab standing in the file, matching the record's lime tab.
   - **Hub scale.** The file is 150 wide and 138 tall in healthtech's units (was 200 × 186): a little taller
     than a pawn, as the hub's medical case is. People are healthtech's pawn (R 22, H 62, head 16) and arm
     (11 including its 1.6 blue outline, hand 0.8 of that), and the plate is healthtech's.
   - **The handover is the action** (the blind reader saw HR/CRM "filing"). The scene now runs on one line
     along the floor's y axis: the file at the back, the person standing at its pocket who drew the record
     out, the record held between two hands, the next person further out taking it, the lime tab leading.
     Both people are client figures (white, light arms outlined in blue): the handover is between two roles
     of the client's own team, which is what workflow software carries; no Tcules figure is needed for that
     idea. Arms grow from the shoulder (the outline masked inside the body), same weight both sides.
   - **Arrangement.** No sibling shares it: object at the back-right, a pair in a line coming forward from it,
     the product held by people rather than standing as a device.

   Zoom-checked at 1800px: the giver's hand sits over the record's back edge, the receiver's over its front
   edge, each arm meets its body with no outline seam; the lime tab sits behind the record's top edge; the
   ghost tab lies inside the back board; the giver's head stands clear in front of the pocket's corner.
   At 360: the file with its three role tabs, the record between two people, and the lime tab all read.

## Parts

- Floor: healthtech's plate, dotted, brandeis-50 inset, dashed brandeis-500 edge.
- The hero object: a solid brandeis-500 expanding file at hub scale, an accordion gusset in brandeis-600 with
  brandeis-700 valley folds, a plain white label slip on its pocket.
- Three white dividers with equal tabs: a person, a person, an AI badge.
- The empty slot: a dashed lime outline of the missing sheet's tab, standing in the file.
- The record: a white sheet the size of a divider, owner avatar and title, stage stepper (current stop ringed);
  a lime tab with one ink bar on its leading end.
- Two clients (white pawns, blue outline, light arms outlined in blue, one arm weight): the one at the file
  hands the record on, the next one takes it, each hand on one end.
