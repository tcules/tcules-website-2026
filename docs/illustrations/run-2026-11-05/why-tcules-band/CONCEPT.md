## The idea

**The sentence a buyer would say back:** "Their people sit with ours and decide what to build first, before
building it: tested with real users, tied to the numbers."

One studio table, and round it **three pairs**, one per principle. Each pair is one blue Tcules person beside
one light client person, both working on the same thing. Exactly three blue figures and three client figures,
and nobody else.

## Composition (settled in words before drawing)

reads as one shared studio table, not a strip. Two pairs stand shoulder to shoulder on the front long edge and
one pair behind the middle. **Nobody stands at a short end. In every pair both hands land on one thing.**
