# /why-tcules — "Better product decisions, before you build" (one band-wide drawing)

One wide drawing for the whole band (owner, 5 Nov 2026), replacing the three small principle figures in
`../why-tcules-principles/`. Drawn for the tint band (paper-deep `#f3f1ea`), `viewBox` about 1000 × 525 (1.9:1),
rendered up to about 1100px wide. Source: `band.gen.mjs` → `band.svg`.

## Candidate ideas

1. **A studio table, decided before it is built.** Tcules and the client's team work at one long table around
   the product, which still lies in the middle as a dashed outline with three bays. A real user tries the
   prototype, the first piece is set into the first bay, and the design is wired to the number it should move.
   **(picked)**
2. **A blueprint under a ghost product.** A large blueprint lies on the floor with the product hovering above it
   as a dashed ghost, and three decision marks on the blueprint.
3. **A three-stop path.** A path crosses the floor through a user test, a sorting tray and a rising chart, and
   ends at an unbuilt screen.

**Why 1.** It is literal: people at a table with things on it, no metaphor to decode. All three principles sit
on one base, the way `expertise.svg` puts six industries on one product. The owner's line of thought (a
studio where Tcules and the client work together) becomes the base itself. The dashed product frame in the middle of
the table says "before you build" without words, and its bays carry "what to build first". Idea 2 is a metaphor (a blueprint and a ghost) that needs explaining. Idea 3
is three separate vignettes on a path, which is the pilot's "too simple, three small figures" problem again.

**The sentence a buyer would say back:** "They work out with us what to build, and which piece first, before
building it: tested with real users and tied to the numbers."

## Rounds

1. Long table, small objects. Read correctly but empty, with one very long arm and the bars hidden.
2. Shorter, deeper table, but the pinboard at the back left pushed the picture to 1.7:1 and the laptop covered
   the "later" outline.
3. Board moved to face the table from the left, longer zones, bars on a base at the back right of their zone.
4. Board and researcher moved beside the user, so evidence reads as one corner. Added the paper sketch,
   notebook and mug. Floor tightened.
5. Zoom-check fixes: the wire ran exactly along the white bars' tops (an accidental tangent), so the laptop's
   button moved to the top right of its screen and the wire now clears them. The researcher's hand moved off
   the tick to the note's corner.
6. Fix pass after the blind read ("one team designs, builds and measures"; the lime cube meant nothing; arms
   read as leashes; the ghost screen read as unfinished). "Before you build" became the spine: the off-floor
   ghost screen is gone and the unbuilt product now lies ON the table in the middle, one dashed frame whose
   three bays are first, next and later, so principle 2 sits inside the heading's idea. The lime block shrank
   to a bay's size; the separate faded box that overlapped it is gone. The designer stands close behind bay 1
   with a short arm angled down onto the block. The laptop screen shows a design (picture, title, one button),
   not an editor; the bars moved beside it and forward, and the wire now starts on a dot on the button and
   runs dotted along the x axis to the tallest bar's top, on the pale zone, clear of the table rim and the
   floor's edge (at the first try it projected exactly onto the table's back rim and vanished: moved the
   laptop forward). Table shortened and made shallower, floor's front cropped, board and researcher pulled in
   toward the phone with the ticked note kept clear of the researcher's head. Stroke widths rounded to one
   decimal in the generator.

## Open for the owner

- The ratio is about 1.9:1, just under the 2:1 to 3:1 brief. An isometric floor diamond's bounding box is always
  2:1. Going wider would mean cropping the floor's tips, which no approved hero does.
- The front of the floor is now cropped to the people standing in front of the table (about a seventh of the
  height at the right end, where the floor's corner falls).
- The blind reader has to be re-run on this round (fix 8): pass when a cold reader says something close to
  "they work out with the client what to build, and which piece first, before building it". If it still comes
  back as "one team designs, builds and measures", the next step is a redraw with the product frame as the
  hero object and the three activities around it.
