# /tools · hero

Slot: the hero beside the copy on /tools, paper ground, renders about 560–620px wide.
`hero.svg`, viewBox 860 × 650 (about 1.32:1), built by `gen.mjs` (`node gen.mjs` rewrites `hero.svg`).
It uses the healthtech floor (545 × 402, the same projection, dots, inset and dashed edge) and the healthtech pawn
and arm geometry, so it sits at the family's scale. Not an expertise hero: it follows the approved heroes.

## Candidate ideas

1. **Question in, checklist worked, brief out.** A client with a question works down a big clipboard checklist, and
   a printed brief with a lime action lies on the floor in front of it.
2. **Pick one of six.** Six worksheets laid out on the floor with one slot empty, and the picked one on a clipboard
   in the client's hands.
3. **A funnel.** A question card drops into a tray, passes a row of ticks and comes out as a brief.

**Pick: 1, with 2 folded in as the supporting detail.** Idea 1 is the copy's job in one look and uses real objects
(a clipboard, a printed brief). The six laid-out sheets with one empty dashed slot add "pick the one that matches
your question" without a second idea: they sit to the left of the client, the clipboard is the missing one. Idea 3
is a machine metaphor that needs explaining.

## The drawing

- **Idea:** "Pick the checklist for your question, work through it, and walk away with a brief your team can act on."
- **Drawn:** the dotted floor with a dashed edge and a soft inset. On the left, five small worksheets lie on the inset
  in a two-by-three set with one dashed empty slot; a dashed line (iso axes, turning once) runs from that slot to the
  foot of the clipboard's stand, so the set reads as "this sheet was taken from here". The hero object is a solid
  blue clipboard in a low white stand, a small white clip gripping the top of its white sheet: a blue question tile
  (a white bar with a dot under it, no glyph) and a title bar at its head, then five checklist rows, four ticked in
  blue, the last one empty under the client's hand. The white client pawn stands at the board, its short light arm
  (blue outline) reaching from the shoulder to that last box. From a dot on the sheet just under the last row, a
  second dashed line (1.6, white halo where it crosses the blue frame) runs forward over the stand and drops onto the
  back edge of a printed brief with a clear arrowhead. The brief lies front right, the second-largest object: a blue
  title bar, text bars and one short lime action button (the drawing's one accent).
- **Revisions:** (1) the first pass put the client far off with a long stick arm and the brief stranded at the
  floor's front tip; the client moved to the board so the arm is short, and the brief moved under the board.
  (2) The brief tried the right corner, where it crowded the floor edge; it went to the front, larger, its header
  changed from a blue band (it read as a button) to a title bar. (3) The front-left floor was empty; the six
  worksheets went there, giving the "pick one" beat. (4) Polish: the stand no longer pokes out beside the client,
  the worksheets were enlarged to read at 600, the arrow stops short of the brief, the viewBox was trimmed.
  (5) Fix pass after the blind read ("three objects on a floor", "the person looks confused"): the scene is now one
  chain, pick → work → brief. A connector joins the empty slot to the clipboard; the output connector starts on the
  sheet, not the stand, at object weight with a larger arrowhead; the brief is 1.3x larger and forward right, its lime
  pill shortened to half the sheet so it reads as one action; the speech bubble is gone (the question lives on the
  sheet, the client works it); the "?" glyph is replaced by a glyph-free tile; the clip is 20% smaller and grips the
  sheet; the worksheet set sits fully on the inset. Both connectors and the lime button hold at 360.
- **Deviation to flag:** none left. The question tile is glyph-free; if the owner wants a "?" back, it is one
  glyph on that tile only (STYLE §1).
